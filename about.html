01 / My story

Hi, my name is Angelina Delalu, a student at Miva Open University studying Computer Science. I am from a small town in Imo State, Nigeria. My passion for technology grew as I discovered its potential for automation, problem-solving and improving lives. This inspired me to keep learning and explore how technology can create meaningful solutions to real-world challenges.

Career aspirations

My career aspiration is to become a Solution Architect specializing in Microsoft Power Platform, AI, and web development. I aim to build innovative applications, create responsive websites and automate business processes to solve real-world problems. I also hope to share my knowledge and mentor aspiring tech professionals, especially those with limited access to technology opportunities.
